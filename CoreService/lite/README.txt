MO TA:
  Cai tren may co camera de giam sat tu xa.
  Server tu chay nen, mo port, khong can thao tac gi.

YEU CAU:
  - Node.js (https://nodejs.org)

CAI DAT:
  1. Click phai "install.bat" → "Run as administrator"
  2. Xong! Server tu dong chay.

GIAM SAT TU XA:
  Tu iPhone hoac may tinh khac cung mang:
  - Manager: http://<ip>:4200/manager (PIN: 1621)
  - Camera:  http://<ip>:4200/

WEBCAM LAPTOP:
  Mo open_emitter.bat de phat webcam cua laptop nay.
  Lan dau: cap quyen camera, lan sau tu dong.

KET HOP VOI BAN FULL:
  - May o nha:    Cai ban LITE (may nay)
  - May co quan:  Cai ban FULL hoac mo browser
  - iPhone:       Mo Safari → http://<ip>:4200/manager

GO CAI DAT:
  Click phai "uninstall.bat" → "Run as administrator"
