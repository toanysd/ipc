  NETWORK DEVICE MANAGER - FULL
  Ung dung quan ly thiet bi mang doc lap
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

YEU CAU:
  - Node.js (https://nodejs.org)

CAI DAT:
  1. Click phai "install.bat" → "Run as administrator"
  2. Chon che do:
     - Mode 1: Chi giao dien quan ly (mac dinh)
       Khong mo port, chi truy cap qua localhost
     - Mode 2: Day du + mo port firewall
       Thiet bi khac co the ket noi qua IP
  3. Xong! Server tu chay khi bat may.

SU DUNG:
  manager.bat        - Mo giao dien quan ly
  open_dashboard.bat - Mo Camera Dashboard
  open_emitter.bat   - Mo trang phat Webcam

TRUY CAP TU XA (chi khi cai Mode 2):
  Manager: http://<ip>:4200/manager
  Camera:  http://<ip>:4200/
  PIN: 1621

KET HOP VOI BAN LITE:
  - May o nha (co camera): Cai ban LITE de mo port
  - May quan ly (o co quan): Cai ban FULL (Mode 1)
  - Ket noi: Manager tren may FULL → camera tren may LITE

GO CAI DAT:
  Click phai "uninstall.bat" → "Run as administrator"
